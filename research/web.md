## General UK context

Not facts about KensalBakes.

Blue Light Card (UK):
- A membership scheme for eligible public-service workers. Search summaries list NHS staff (including admin and support staff), emergency services, social care workers and armed forces members as eligible. NHS Trust and Health Board volunteers can also apply with valid NHS ID.
- Discounts are offered by individual retailers or brands. Whether a given bakery offers one is set by that business, not by the scheme.
- One search summary gives membership as £4.99 for two years. Check the current price and rules on the official site.
- Sources: https://www.bluelightcard.co.uk/en/discounts-for-nhs-workers ; https://becleverwithyourcash.com/blue-light-card-deals-and-offers/ ; https://www.householdmoneysaving.com/who-qualifies-for-a-blue-light-card/
